# Product vision

## Problem

A consulting engineer working across several jobs, customers, languages, and technical projects needs one reliable place to answer five questions:

1. What needs attention today?
2. What commitments emerged from meetings or messages?
3. What concrete progress was made on each deliverable?
4. What is blocked, late, or at risk?
5. Where did the working time go?

Existing information is fragmented across calendars, conversations, documents, and personal notes. The product should reduce missed commitments without creating another high-maintenance planning system.

## Product promise

Jonathan AI PM converts daily signals into a controlled project loop: plan the day, capture commitments, advance deliverables in small steps, close the day, and update project health.

## Primary user

A multi-client consulting professional managing concurrent technical projects, meetings, deadlines, and documents in Spanish, Portuguese, and English.

## Outcomes

- A concise morning view of meetings, deadlines, priorities, and risks.
- Fast capture of tasks and action items with traceability to their origin.
- Visible incremental progress on MOPs, designs, assessments, and similar deliverables.
- A dependable evening reconciliation of completed, deferred, and blocked work.
- Effort and delivery metrics by workspace/job, client, project, and deliverable.
- A structure another collaborator can understand without relying on oral history.

## Product principles

- **Low friction:** capture first; enrich only what is necessary.
- **Traceable:** retain the relationship from meeting to action to task and deliverable.
- **Human controlled:** AI proposes; the user confirms changes that affect commitments.
- **Progress over activity:** prioritize deliverable movement, not a long task count.
- **Privacy by design:** minimum data, explicit boundaries, and no secrets in source control.
- **Multilingual:** preserve original language and optionally provide a working translation.

## Phase 0 scope

Phase 0 defines structure, terminology, operating flow, configuration boundaries, and synthetic data. It deliberately excludes user-facing automation and production integrations.

## Future non-goals

- Replacing enterprise project-management or ticketing systems.
- Sending messages or accepting commitments without user confirmation.
- Storing unrestricted customer configurations, credentials, or confidential transcripts.
- Treating AI-generated status as authoritative without evidence from project records.

## Success indicators

- Morning Brief can be reviewed in under five minutes.
- New commitments can be captured in under one minute.
- Every open action item has an owner, due date or review date, and next destination.
- Evening Close leaves no captured item unclassified.
- Project status can be reconstructed from linked tasks, deliverables, meetings, and work logs.
